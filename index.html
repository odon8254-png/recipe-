waffles

by donte

Waffles are a beloved breakfast classic characterized by their distinct grid pattern, which perfectly holds pools of melted butter and sweet maple syrup. Made from a simple batter of flour, eggs, milk, and a leavening agent, they are cooked between two hot, iron plates until the exterior turns golden brown and crispy while the inside remains light and fluffy. Beyond the traditional style, variations like the thick, deep-pocketed Belgian waffle or the yeast-risen Liege waffle with caramelized pearl sugar offer diverse textures and flavors. Whether piled high with fresh berries and whipped cream for a decadent brunch or paired with savory fried chicken, waffles serve as a versatile canvas for both sweet and savory culinary creations.
